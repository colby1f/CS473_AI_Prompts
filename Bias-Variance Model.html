How to read the target:
  • Bias is represented by how far the center of the predictions is from the bullseye.
  • Variance is represented by how spread out the individual predictions are.
  • Low bias + low variance: predictions are tightly clustered around the center.
  • High bias + low variance: predictions are tightly clustered, but away from the center.
  • Low bias + high variance: predictions are spread out around the center.
  • High bias + high variance: predictions are both spread out and centered away from the target.
